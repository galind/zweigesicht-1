It records visible surface character, not measured material constants or manufacturing proof. All numerical shader parameters remain authored.

## Variant boundary

The visible reference movement is the shock-indicator configuration. Marco Lang and SJX both describe the shock indicator as optional; without it, an engraved plate occupies that position. Quill & Pad reports that examples may be personalized in materials, engraving, dials, and finishing. Case versions in steel, rose gold, and platinum therefore do not prove a single immutable movement finish. The CAD includes the shock module and overlapping setting-spring alternatives. Treat the photographed `00/18` configuration as the finish target for the current movement view, not as proof that every delivered watch or CAD alternative is identical.

The maker JPEG is a studio photograph rather than a CAD render: it has camera EXIF, photographic depth/reflection behavior, and an editing history. It is heavily processed and isolated on black, so it is strong for topology and relative finish contrast but unsuitable for calibrated color or roughness. The SJX 2023 photographs show optical depth of field, crystal reflections, and different illumination and are treated as independent photographic corroboration. The maker MP4 and exploded shock PNG are renders/presentation media.

## Rendering priorities

1. Match the whole hierarchy in `REF-MAKER-01`: warm granular plate, cool straight-grained bridge fields, thin polished bevel bands, selective blue, transparent jewels, and warm wheels. This relative hierarchy matters more than exact sampled color.
2. Add face-region separation to representative bridges before broadening procedural noise. The macro evidence calls for at least top field, polished bevel/countersink, frosted base/foot, and recessed blue border.
3. Preserve steel on the crown assembly and map its blue cone precisely to `d_0_1_1_251` face 22, as established by the subsequent CAD audit. The toothed annulus `249` remains steel.
4. Keep blue screw heads, neutral polished pins, steel screws, and brass pins as distinct assignments. Shared definitions may require instance overrides.
5. Make jewels optically deep and keep their chatons separate. The empty diamond remains an explicit source limitation.
6. Recheck `d_0_1_1_110` against the warm photographed balance rim. The evidence supports a visual review, not an alloy claim.

Do not derive absolute roughness, anisotropy, normal strength, color, or texture frequency from these photographs. Studio processing, lens and crystal reflections, white balance, and downsampling all alter apparent values. Tune authored parameters by matched-angle comparison and label them as emulation.
