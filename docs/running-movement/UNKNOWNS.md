External reviewer checklist, in priority order:

1. Annotate the source-rest escapement pose and a full two-sided lock–unlock–impulse–drop sequence, including roller/fork/safety and banking. Supply a justified amplitude/phase range.
2. Confirm the spring's exact inner/outer attachment frames and permissible deformation model, reviewing the source-tolerance findings rather than assuming CAD precision.
3. Draw the two-barrel drum/arbor/ratchet/spring coupling graph for normal running and winding; address the ratchet offset and winding-state interval.
4. Confirm the cross-face transfer shaft connection and the front cannon/seconds hand couplings, then review both display directions.
5. Review source variants, nominally fixed hosts, possible back-driven keyless parts and proposed tolerances. Record mechanical, user-visual and device qualifications as separate approvals.
